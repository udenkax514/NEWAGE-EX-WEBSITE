Architecture

One company. Two layers of work.

NEWAGE-EX is the parent company. NEWAGE Browser is the current commercial software product. Axiom Technologies is a division of NEWAGE-EX — the advanced technology, infrastructure, hardware and R&D arm.

Axiom is not a replacement for the company, and it is not presented as an independent parent. Same company. Different layers of the same work.

Why this structure

Different products. One direction.

NEWAGE Browser establishes the company's software foundation. Axiom extends that foundation toward computing, infrastructure, hardware and advanced engineering.

The browser is not a side project, and the longer research horizon is not a separate company. They are sequential layers of one trajectory: build the foundation, then build beyond it.
