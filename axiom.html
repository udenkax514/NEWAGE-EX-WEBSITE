Axiom Technologies

Engineering the systems behind the next generation of computing. Axiom is the advanced technology, infrastructure, hardware and R&D division of NEWAGE-EX — the long-term engineering arm of the company.

Status

Research first.

Axiom does not yet have a standalone commercial product. Development is being approached progressively — research, supplier relationships, prototyping and technical validation come first.

It is a division of NEWAGE-EX, not a separate company. An early, exploratory direction includes semiconductors and electronics.
