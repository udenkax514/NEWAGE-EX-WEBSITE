The product

Where the NEWAGE-EX vision becomes software.

NEWAGE Browser is NEWAGE-EX's first major commercial software product — a Chromium-based Windows desktop browser built around privacy, local AI execution, browser automation, controlled browser execution, complex web workflows, security, isolation, and intelligent interaction with web environments.

Computers shouldn't just understand what users want. They should be able to execute it.

It is not a chatbot placed beside a browser. The AI operates the browser itself through a controlled execution layer. It can navigate real websites, click, type, scroll and switch tabs — observing each page and verifying its own actions as it works through a task.

It also keeps a persistent personal memory — information you choose to have it remember, retrieved when relevant and still there the next time you open the browser. It is stored locally and used only at your direction.
